### Style notes — what to borrow

**Consequence chains without fanfare:**
The church/plague/guilds sequence is a masterclass in letting a chain of consequences speak for
itself. No "and this is significant because." The next sentence is just the next consequence.
> "When plagues would hit, the church explained it as too much cousin marriage.
> The breakdown of clan families into nuclear households inadvertently created voluntary associations.
> Charter towns. Universities. Guilds."

**Expectation + quiet reversal:**
Henrich sets up a clear prior ("I thought they'd show similar results") then delivers the
contradiction flatly, without drama. The flat delivery is the technique.
> "I thought the Macha Ganga would show similar results. But they didn't feel inclined to reject at all. And people gave pretty low offers."

**Short sentence as the beat after a long one:**
The ultimatum game passage earns "If they reject, both players get 0." because of the long
setup before it. The short sentence only lands because the ear has been waiting.

**Specificity as credibility:**
"Steve Hyna and Oran Noren Zion" / "University of the South Pacific" / "Zurich" — real names
and places do the work of establishing authority. No abstract claim needed.

### Style notes — what to borrow

**Metaphor that earns its strangeness:**
The water park / lazy river analogy works because it's committed to fully. The speaker doesn't
hedge ("sort of like" / "in a way"). They say "as one does" — the deadpan acknowledges the
absurdity and keeps going. The image lands because it's concrete enough to follow step by step.
> "If you think of the inside of your body as a water park as one does, the lymphatic system
> is like the lazy river. These baby hookworms fall into this little lymphatic river. Then they
> float along. I picture them with cozies of non-alcoholic beer, floating downstream."

**Escalating specificity as comedy:**
"No various flavors of no." — the phrase earns its punch because of the setup: contacted every
lab in the world, all said the same thing, then the landing is a joke that's also completely true.
The joke IS the information.
> "I contacted every laboratory supply company in the world. They all said the same thing.
> No. Various flavors of no."

**Deadpan undercut in the outro:**
"In 20 years." / "20 years." / "No, just next week." — three lines, two speakers, one reversal.
No setup needed. The comedy comes entirely from the flat delivery of the correction.
This is the single-host equivalent: set an absurd expectation in one line, correct it in the next.

**Physical consequence chain:**
The hookworm body journey passage moves entirely through physical actions — enters skin, skin
closes, travels through lymphatics, shot into bloodstream. No abstraction. Each sentence is
the next thing that happens to the worm. The listener can follow without knowing any biology.
